# PRODUCT.md — PolyEcho

register: product

## Product purpose

A personal, single-operator Polymarket copy-trading **instrument**: discover
"smart-money" leaders, backtest them with slippage/latency models, copy-trade
with full risk controls, and monitor it all. Self-hosted, private keys never
leave the machine. Not a SaaS, not a hype product. See `docs/PRD.md`.

## Users

One user: **Shan**. IT-infra background. Risk-averse, values
**explainability and safety** over flash. Real money is involved
($500–$2,000+ USDC). Uses it on a 27" Mac in a home office (daytime, primary)
and occasionally glances at alerts on iPhone (evening). Often *glancing*
(today's PnL, open risk, alerts), sometimes *acting* (pause a leader, queue a
backtest, follow/reject a candidate).

## Brand & tone

PolyEcho.
Voice: calm, precise, factual, trustworthy. A quiet, dependable instrument,
not a casino. Numbers must be legible at a glance and never lie. Confidence
through restraint, not decoration.

## Strategic principles

- **Legibility first.** Money on screen: tabular figures, clear gain/loss
  semantics (never color-only), unambiguous state. A misread number is a bug.
- **Calm over exciting.** No dopamine UI. The dashboard should feel like a
  well-made cockpit gauge cluster, not a trading-app growth funnel.
- **Honest empty/loading/error states.** Fixture-first; pages are often
  genuinely empty. Say so plainly with the real next action.
- **Read-only by default; writes are deliberate.** Safe writes (follow/pause,
  settings, enqueue backtest) are explicit; dangerous ops are CLI-only.

## Anti-references (do NOT look like these)

- Crypto/DeFi neon-on-black hype dashboards. (First-order category reflex —
  banned.)
- Fintech navy + gold "premium" skin. (Same reflex, one tier over — banned.)
- The SaaS hero-metric template: one giant number + 3 supporting stat cards +
  gradient accent. Banned outright.
- Identical icon+heading+text card grids.
- Bloomberg-terminal cosplay (dense green monospace on black for its own sake).
